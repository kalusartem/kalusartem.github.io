Privacy policy: Channel Analytics

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Who uses this app

Channel Analytics is a personal tool used only by its owner, for YouTube channels the owner manages. It isn't offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, through Google sign-in, it reads:

  • the channel's own video list and video details (titles, upload filenames, publish status, durations, view counts), through the YouTube Data API (youtube.readonly);
  • the channel's analytics (views, watch time, average view duration, subscribers gained), through the YouTube Analytics API (yt-analytics.readonly).

Both scopes are read-only. The app can't upload, change or delete any content.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to show the owner their own statistics and to warn them before posting a video that is already uploaded. Sign-in tokens and results stay on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's APIs. The data isn't sold or shared, and it isn't used for advertising or to train machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Channel Analytics' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to data Google processes.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local token files also removes its access.
